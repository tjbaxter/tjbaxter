### Problems I work on in real-time voice

- **Turn-taking.** Telling a finished sentence from a caller pausing halfway through an address.
- **Barge-in.** Stopping the moment the caller cuts in, and listening.
- **Tool calls with a caller waiting.** Finding a slot and writing the booking into the calendar or CRM the business already runs, before the call ends.
- **Behaviour pinned in snapshot tests.** A wording change shows up as a diff before it ships.
